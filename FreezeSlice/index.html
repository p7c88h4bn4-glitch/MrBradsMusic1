Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how FreezeSlice, provided by Mr. Brad's Music, handles information when you use the app, including its audio effect extension in a compatible host app.

Your audio stays with you. FreezeSlice processes audio on your device. It does not require an account, collect personal information, include ads or analytics, or upload audio to us.

Information collection and tracking

FreezeSlice does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or developer-operated cloud services. We do not maintain a user database or track your activity across apps or websites.

Audio processing

Audio supplied to FreezeSlice is processed locally to provide its audio effects. Audio held in memory for effects such as freezing or slicing is used for that processing and is not sent to the developer or a remote server.

When you use FreezeSlice within a host app, that app supplies audio and may save or record its output. The host app's own privacy policy and settings apply to its handling of that audio.

Settings, presets, and saved data

Settings and presets saved by FreezeSlice remain on your device or in a host app's project. We do not receive them. If your device or host app includes this data in a backup or synchronization service, that service operates according to your settings and its own privacy policy.

Permissions

If an audio feature requires microphone access, access is controlled by the operating system and your permission settings. Audio used by FreezeSlice is processed locally. You can change microphone permission in your device's Settings. A host app may request its own permissions separately.

Apple and other services

Apple handles App Store downloads and any purchases through its systems. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may separately process store information or diagnostics under its privacy policy and your device settings. Host apps and other services you choose to use have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

We retain no personal information from your use of FreezeSlice because the app does not send it to us. Removing the app removes its app-local data through the operating system. Copies saved in host projects, exported files, or backups may remain until you remove them separately.

Children's privacy

FreezeSlice does not collect personal information from users of any age, including children.

Contacting us

For privacy questions, contact Mr. Brad's Music at (727) 259-4664. If you contact us, we receive only the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your request. This voluntary correspondence is separate from the app's operation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FreezeSlice changes. The current policy will be available on this page, with its effective date updated when changes are made.
