🎶 The Mr. Brad Tour

A high-energy live music experience created especially for young children and families.

👶 Ages: 0–6 and families
⏱️ Program Length: 60 minutes
🎟️ Performance Fee: $675
🛠️ Setup Time: Approximately 1½ hours
📦 Breakdown Time: Approximately 1 hour

🎵 About the Program

The Mr. Brad Show is a high-energy live music experience created especially for young children and families.

The show combines live music, singing, movement, comedy and audience participation. Children are encouraged to sing, dance, listen, move and experience the excitement of seeing and hearing a wide variety of musical instruments performed live.

🎸 Instruments

Brad performs on guitar, violin, mandolin, banjo, flute, various drums, piano, synthesizers, glockenspiel, sitar, percussion and musical toys—to name a few.

🎸 🎻 🪕 🪈 🥁 🎹 🎛️ 🔔 🪘 🎶 🧸 ✨

🎯 Purpose

The primary goal of The Mr. Brad Show is simple: give young children the opportunity to see lots of different musical instruments in action and inspire them to someday pick one up themselves.

Playing an instrument is an extraordinary combination of listening, movement, timing, coordination, memory, creativity and self-expression. Experiencing real instruments up close can open a door for a child that may stay open for the rest of their life.

Mr. Brad’s larger mission is to inspire as many people as possible to discover the joy of making music and to carry that creativity with them throughout their lives.

The show also creates a shared musical experience for children, parents and caregivers—singing, laughing, moving and making memories together. ❤️🎶

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Audience

The program is designed primarily for children ages 0–6 and their families or caregivers.

It works particularly well for libraries, early-childhood programs, preschools, schools, community events and family audiences.

🕺 Capacity

There is no fixed audience capacity imposed by the program.

Capacity can be determined by the venue based on the size and safety limits of the performance space. An open audience area where children have room to move, dance and participate is ideal.

🔊 Production

The Mr. Brad Show is designed to be largely self-contained.

Brad provides:

  • 🎸 Instruments
  • 🔊 Professional-quality sound system
  • 💡 Stage lighting
  • 🎛️ Performance equipment

The venue generally only needs to provide access to electrical power and an appropriate performance space.

📐 Space Requirements

Approximately 10’ x 12’ of performance space is preferred.

Indoor performances are preferred. Outdoor spaces work well as long as there is a solid performance surface and adequate protection from the elements.

An open area in front of the performance space where children can move and dance works particularly well. 💃🕺

🛠️ Setup & Breakdown

Please allow approximately 1½ hours before the performance for setup and sound check.

Breakdown takes approximately 1 hour.

📦 Materials

No materials are required from participants, and there is no materials fee.

📅 Availability

Available year-round for weekday and weekend performances.

Multiple performances in the same geographic area can often be scheduled together, including performances at several library branches or other locations during the same trip.

🚐 Travel

Travel arrangements depend on location and the number of performances scheduled.

For multi-performance engagements, travel costs can often be reduced or eliminated by grouping performances within the same trip.
