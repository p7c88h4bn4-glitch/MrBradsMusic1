🎵 About the Program

The Mr. Brad Show is a high-energy live music experience created especially for young children and families.

The show combines live music, singing, movement, comedy and audience participation. Children are encouraged to sing, dance, listen, move and experience the excitement of seeing and hearing a wide variety of musical instruments performed live.

🎯 Purpose

Give young children the chance to see lots of different instruments in action — and inspire them to someday pick one up themselves.

Playing an instrument combines listening, movement, timing, coordination, memory, creativity and self-expression. Experiencing real instruments up close can open a door for a child that stays open for life.

The show also creates a shared musical experience for children, parents and caregivers — singing, laughing, moving and making memories together. ❤️🎶

What sets this show apart

It arrives as a real concert.

Not a performer with an acoustic guitar and a portable speaker. Brad travels with a full professional sound and lighting rig, sets it up himself, mixes it himself and strikes it himself. For most of the children in the room, this is the first real concert they have ever been to — and it looks and sounds like one.

🎚️ Sound System

Every show is mixed through a professional-quality sound system chosen specifically for rooms full of young children.

  • Full-range professional PAClean, warm, even coverage — children in the back hear as well as children in the front.
  • Professional microphonesVocals, instruments and audience participation all come through clearly.
  • Digital mixing and effectsEvery instrument balanced and sound-checked before the doors open.
  • Volume set for little earsRun at a comfortable, child-safe level — big and exciting, never overwhelming.

💡 Stage Lighting

A complete stage lighting package travels with the show, turning an ordinary meeting room or storytime space into a genuine performance space.

  • Colorful stage washesWarm, bright color that makes the performance area feel like a stage.
  • Scene changes through the showLighting shifts with the music — quiet songs, big dance numbers and everything between.
  • Child-friendly designNo strobing, no harsh flashing, nothing aimed into the audience.
  • Self-contained setupRuns from standard wall power; set up and removed by Brad.
