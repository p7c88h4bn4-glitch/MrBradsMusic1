🚀 Musical Spaceship

A live music-and-science adventure through the solar system.

🧑‍🚀 Ages: 5–11 and families
⏱️ Program Length: Approximately 60 minutes
🎟️ Performance Fee: $850
🛠️ Setup Time: Approximately 2 hours
📦 Breakdown Time: Approximately 1 hour

🌌 About the Program

Musical Spaceship is a live music-and-science adventure that takes children on an imaginative journey through the solar system.

The audience boards an imaginary spaceship and travels from world to world while exploring planets, moons, gravity, light, space exploration, music, mathematics and the remarkable patterns found throughout the universe.

Along the way, original songs, live instruments, comedy, storytelling and real space imagery turn the program into much more than a science presentation—it becomes an adventure. 🚀🎶🪐

🖥️ The Spaceship Window

At the center of the show is a large television display that becomes the viewing window of the spaceship.

Mr. Brad has created an entire movie-style visual presentation using imagery and video from NASA and ESA.

As the spaceship travels through the solar system, the window reveals the actual places we are visiting: the surface of planets, enormous storms, strange moons, spacecraft views, scientific visualizations and some of the most spectacular images ever captured beyond Earth.

Rather than simply hearing about the solar system, the audience experiences the program as though we are traveling through it together.

🎸 Music & Instruments

Live music is woven throughout the entire journey.

Brad performs on guitar, violin, banjo, flute, various drums, piano, synthesizers, glockenspiel, percussion and musical toys—to name a few.

Original themed songs appear throughout the adventure, helping give each destination its own musical identity.

🎯 Purpose

The goal of Musical Spaceship is to make science feel enormous, exciting and personal.

Children encounter real images and ideas from planetary science while experiencing them through music, storytelling and imagination.

The program encourages curiosity about:

  • 🔭 Astronomy
  • 🪐 Planetary science
  • 🚀 Space exploration
  • 🎵 Music
  • ➗ Mathematics
  • 🌊 Patterns, rhythm and resonance
  • 💡 Light and motion
  • 🌌 The scale and wonder of the universe

The goal is not simply for children to remember a list of facts.

It is for them to leave wanting to know what else is out there. ✨

🎼 Music, Math & the Universe

One of the themes running through the program is the connection between music, mathematics and patterns in nature.

Rhythm, vibration, frequency, orbital motion and repeating patterns give children another way to think about both music and science.

Even the movements of moons and planets can create repeating relationships that feel surprisingly musical.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Audience

The program is designed primarily for children ages 5–11 and their families.

The show works well for libraries, elementary schools, museums, science centers, community programs and family events.

👀 Capacity

There is no fixed audience capacity imposed by the program.

Capacity can be determined by the venue based on the size and safety limits of the performance space.

Because the spaceship window is an important part of the experience, audience members should have reasonable sightlines to the display.

🔊 Production

Musical Spaceship is designed to be a largely self-contained production.

Brad provides:

  • 🎸 Musical instruments
  • 🔊 Professional-quality sound system
  • 💡 Stage lighting
  • 🖥️ Large television/display
  • 🚀 Spaceship visual presentation
  • 🎛️ Playback and performance equipment

The venue generally needs to provide an appropriate performance space and access to electrical power.

The visual element of this show also requires a large TV screen or projector with HDMI connectivity, preferably existing above the stage area.

📐 Space Requirements

Approximately 10’ x 12’ of performance space is preferred.

The audience should have a clear view of the spaceship window throughout the program.

A room where the lighting can be reduced somewhat enhances the visual experience and helps transform the performance area into the interior of the spaceship. 🌌

Indoor performances are strongly preferred for this program because of the visual presentation.

🛠️ Setup & Breakdown

Please allow approximately 2 hours before the performance for setup, visual preparation and sound check.

Breakdown takes approximately 1 hour.

📦 Materials

No materials are required from participants, and there is no materials fee.

No printed materials need to be distributed during the program.

📅 Availability

Available year-round for weekday and weekend performances.

Multiple performances in the same geographic area can often be scheduled together, including performances at several library branches, schools or other venues during the same trip.

🚐 Travel

Travel arrangements depend on location and the number of performances scheduled.

For multi-performance engagements, travel costs can often be reduced or eliminated by grouping performances within the same trip.
