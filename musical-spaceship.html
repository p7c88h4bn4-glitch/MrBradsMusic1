🌌 About the Program

Musical Spaceship is a live music-and-science adventure that takes children on an imaginative journey through the solar system.

The audience boards an imaginary spaceship and travels from world to world while exploring planets, moons, gravity, light, space exploration, music, mathematics and the remarkable patterns found throughout the universe.

Original songs, live instruments, comedy, storytelling and real space imagery turn the program into much more than a science presentation — it becomes an adventure. 🚀🎶🪐

🎯 Purpose

The goal is to make science feel enormous, exciting and personal. Children encounter real images and ideas from planetary science while experiencing them through music, storytelling and imagination.

The program sparks curiosity about:

  • Astronomy
  • Planetary science
  • Space exploration
  • Music
  • Mathematics
  • Patterns & rhythm
  • Light and motion
  • Scale of the universe

The goal isn't a list of facts to memorize. It's that they leave wanting to know what else is out there. ✨

The heart of the show

🖥️ The Spaceship Window

At the center of the show is a large display that becomes the viewing window of the spaceship — and Mr. Brad has built an entire movie-style visual presentation to fill it, using imagery and video from NASA and ESA.

As the spaceship travels, the window reveals the actual places we are visiting: the surfaces of planets, enormous storms, strange moons, spacecraft views, scientific visualizations and some of the most spectacular images ever captured beyond Earth.

The visuals are cued live to the music, scene by scene, so the imagery, the songs and the story all move together. Rather than simply hearing about the solar system, the audience experiences the program as though we are traveling through it together.

🎸 Music & Instruments

Live music is woven throughout the entire journey. Brad performs on guitar, violin, banjo, flute, drums, piano, synthesizers, glockenspiel, percussion and musical toys — to name a few.

Original themed songs appear throughout the adventure, giving each destination its own musical identity.

🎼 Music, Math & the Universe

One of the themes running through the program is the connection between music, mathematics and patterns in nature.

Rhythm, vibration, frequency, orbital motion and repeating patterns give children another way to think about both music and science.

Even the movements of moons and planets create repeating relationships that feel surprisingly musical.

Production

A self-contained production

Brad handles all sound, lighting, visual playback and cueing.

🚀 Brad brings

  • All musical instruments
  • Professional-quality sound system
  • Microphones, stands and cabling
  • Full stage lighting package
  • The complete spaceship visual presentation
  • Playback, cueing and performance equipment
  • All setup, operation and breakdown

🏛️ The venue provides

  • Access to standard electrical power
  • An appropriate indoor performance space
  • Ideally, a large screen or projector with HDMI
  • A room that can be dimmed, if possible

🖥️ Brad travels with a large display for smaller rooms. For bigger audiences, a house screen or projector with HDMI connectivity — ideally mounted above the stage area — gives the best sightlines.

📐 Space

About 10' x 12' of performance space is preferred, with clear sightlines to the spaceship window throughout.

A room that can be dimmed enhances the visuals dramatically. Indoor performances are strongly preferred.

👀 Audience & Capacity

Designed for ages 5–11 and their families. Works well for libraries, elementary schools, museums, science centers and community programs.

No fixed capacity — but everyone should be able to see the display.

📅 Availability

Year-round, weekdays and weekends.

Multiple performances in the same area can be scheduled together — several branches, schools or venues on one trip.
