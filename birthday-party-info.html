🎵 Birthday Party Information

Mr. Brad's Music

Thank you for considering The Mr. Brad Show for your child's birthday party! My goal is to provide a fun, interactive, and memorable musical experience that keeps children engaged while making the birthday child feel extra special.

What to Expect

The Mr. Brad Show is a high-energy, interactive music performance featuring singing, dancing, audience participation, and a variety of musical instruments. Children are encouraged to sing along, move, laugh, and become part of the show.

Every event is a little different, and I adjust the performance to fit the age range, size of the group, and the overall atmosphere of your celebration.

Performance Space Requirements

To provide the best possible experience, I ask that hosts provide:

  • Access to a standard power outlet
  • A firm, level surface for equipment
  • An indoor performance space whenever possible

Outdoor Events: If the event is outdoors, I require substantial cover from direct sun and weather. Musical instruments and electronic equipment can be damaged by excessive heat, moisture, or unexpected weather conditions, and both performers and guests are more comfortable in a shaded environment.

Setup and Teardown

I prefer to arrive approximately 90 minutes before the performance to allow ample time for unloading, setup, sound checks, and preparation.

Please ensure that the performance area is available during this setup period.

Following the performance, teardown and load-out typically require approximately 45–60 minutes.

Pricing

Birthday party performances start at $325.

Pricing may be higher in certain situations, including:

  • Long-distance travel
  • Extended performance times
  • Difficult load-in conditions
  • Special event requirements

Any additional costs will always be discussed in advance.

Special Birthday Recognition

I am happy to make the birthday child feel like the star of the day. Upon request, I can:

  • Perform a special song for the birthday boy or girl
  • Lead the traditional Happy Birthday song as the cake is brought out at the end of the event
  • Include special recognition and audience participation moments during the show

Booking and Questions

If you have questions about your venue, schedule, age group, or any special requests, please don't hesitate to ask. I am happy to work with families to create a fun and successful celebration.

I look forward to helping make your child's birthday a memorable musical adventure!
